LiveSwap

Live Photo & Video converter for iPhone

LiveSwap turns a video into a Live Photo, or pulls the video out of a Live Photo, without re-encoding. Resolution, frame rate and HDR stay as shot.

Common questions

  • The result says "Re-encoded". A few codecs can't be copied directly into a Live Photo. In that case LiveSwap converts the video and tells you so.
  • A long Live Photo won't work as a wallpaper. iOS limits wallpaper Live Photos to short clips. Trim the clip to a few seconds.
  • Saving fails with a permission message. Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → LiveSwap and allow adding photos.
  • Does LiveSwap upload my media? No. Everything is processed on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
