Privacy Policy

LiveSwap · Last updated November 11, 2026

LiveSwap converts between videos and Live Photos on your device. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

Data we collect

None. LiveSwap does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Your photos and videos

  • All processing happens on your device. Your photos and videos are never uploaded.
  • LiveSwap uses the system photo picker. The app can only access the items you select, not the rest of your library.
  • When you save a result, the app asks for permission to add items to your photo library. It does not ask for permission to read your library.
  • Working copies are kept in the app's temporary folder and are deleted when you choose another item and the next time the app starts.

Network, tracking, and third parties

  • LiveSwap makes no network requests.
  • The app contains no ads, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs.
  • The app does not track you.

Children

LiveSwap does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
